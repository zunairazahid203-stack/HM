📌 Important Notes

  • Admission fee is a one-time payment applicable only in the year of joining.
  • Annual charges include: Lab fees, Library fees, Sports fees, Annual function, and ID card.
  • Tuition fee can be paid monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or annually.
  • 5% discount on annual payment of tuition fee.
  • Sibling discount: 10% on tuition fee for the second child, 15% for the third.
  • Transport facility available at additional cost (₹1,500 - ₹3,000/month based on distance).
  • All fees are subject to revision as per school policy with prior notice.
