Admission Process

Simple 5-step process to secure admission

1️⃣

Online Application

Fill the application form on our website with student and parent details.

2️⃣

Document Submission

Upload birth certificate, previous report card, photos, and transfer certificate.

3️⃣

Entrance Test / Interaction

Appear for the test (Class 1+) or child-parent interaction (Nursery-UKG).

4️⃣

Merit List

Selected students are announced on the merit list published on our website.

5️⃣

Fee Payment

Complete the fee payment online or at the school office to confirm admission.

6️⃣

Welcome to HM!

Collect books, uniform, and ID card. Begin your journey with us!
